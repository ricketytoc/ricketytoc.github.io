About

I work where offensive security meets AI. Most of my time goes into two questions: how would an attacker get in, and how do we know an AI agent doing security work is actually getting it right?

Right now I'm a Cybersecurity Engineer Intern in GovTech Singapore's Cyber Security Group. I triage vulnerabilities reported through the government bug bounty programme, reproducing each finding, explaining the risk to the agencies that own the systems and verifying the fixes. I also built a pipeline that turned 1,700 bug bounty and disclosure reports into an evaluation dataset, so the team can test whether new AI models can reason through real vulnerabilities. Before that I was a Software Engineer Intern at Johnson & Johnson, where I built a multi-agent LangGraph tool that turns natural-language requests into architecture diagrams.

I learn offensively: GIAC GPEN certified after SANS SEC560, OffSec PEN-200 completed with the OSCP exam booked for October 2026, and 40 machines compromised on Hack The Box and OffSec Proving Grounds across Linux, Windows and Active Directory. My double degree in Computer Science and Business (Finance) means I also care about explaining technical risk in terms a business can act on.

Outside work you'll find me on a 10K run, on a tennis, badminton or table tennis court, or catching up on Risky Business, Hard Fork and Bloomberg Tech.
