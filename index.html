About

I work where offensive security meets AI. Most of my time goes into two questions: how would an attacker get in, and how do we know an AI agent doing security work is actually getting it right?

Right now I'm a Cybersecurity Engineer Intern at GovTech Singapore, where I built an evaluation harness to catch regressions in the red team's agentic security workflows. Before that I was a Software Engineer Intern at Johnson & Johnson, where I implemented JWT authentication for internal APIs, improving access control on protected endpoints.

I learn offensively: SANS SEC560 completed, with GPEN and OSCP in progress, and regular Hack The Box labs across Linux, Windows and Active Directory. My double degree in Computer Science and Business (Finance) means I also care about explaining technical risk in terms a business can act on.

Outside work you'll find me on a 10K run, on a tennis, badminton or table tennis court, or catching up on Risky Business, Hard Fork and Bloomberg Tech.
